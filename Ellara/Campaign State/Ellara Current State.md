## Ellara's Current Headspace & Emotional Faultlines

### 1. Desperate Relief About Proctor Eduard — Paired with Quiet Cognitive Dissonance
When Eduard welcomed her back on the Seminary grounds, Ellara's **Insight 26** registered **sincere, fatherly concern** in his offer to talk. Inside his office, he appeared taken aback by her report of living-subject experiments, claimed he believed crystals came from ruins and the northern army was a territorial dispute, blamed **Donnathan Reeves** for stalling his inquiries, and offered rail passage to [[Pharos]] for her safety and study. For someone who confessed in Hýrda that *"this is the only life I have known, and if I believe you, I do not know what would become of me"*, Eduard's warmth gave her profound relief (*"I knew it. I knew I could trust you."*).

Yet the cracks haven't vanished—she just stepped around them:
- Earlier that afternoon, she dismissed **Meri**'s warning to **Ysidor** as *"hearsay"* even though Ellara herself matched Meri's Session 7 description of the jail priest to Eduard.
- Once face-to-face with Eduard, she **never asked** her Session 18 questions about **Hýr**, the censored mountain-splitting name, or Eulogian scripture.
- That same night, meditating with her highland herbs, her own [[Spirit]] felt **unnerved, uncertain, shaken, and sad**.

### 2. Caught Between Secrets on Every Side
Ellara values honesty (*"the first step is to be honest"*) and hates deception, yet she is currently holding secrets from both sides:
- **Withheld from Eduard:** She did not mention Nyx killed **Michelle Faberge** (her eyes flickered down when Eduard noted suspected foul play in the head researcher's death), nor did she name her companions, disclose the Hýrda mine collapse, or reveal that the party holds the titan crystal at Whitman Manor.
- **Withheld from Ysidor, Berridin, and Witty:** Only **Nyx** knows she already went to Eduard that night. Ysidor, Witty, and Berridin do not know she spoke with him, none have seen the physical Pharus writ, and Ysidor does not know she secretly completed her Eulogian last rites during his *Ceremony* (`Deception 17`).

### 3. The Bruise with Ysidor vs. Unexpected Solidarity with Nyx
- **With Ysidor:** Her closest spiritual dialogue partner from Hýrda is suddenly strained. His refusal to allow Eulogian rites over the titan crystal (*"Maybe, but they didn't feel welcome when they were alive. I don't think they'll feel very welcome when they're dead"*) cut directly against her creed that *"The light is for everybody."*
- **With Nyx:** The assassin she recoiled from in Session 5 became her clearest emotional anchor in Session 21. Over morning coffee, Nyx admitted sharing her fear, acknowledged almost everyone he has ever known betrayed him, worried that Pharus would only protect *her* (not Shadowed/outcast allies), and called Ellara the group's **level-headed member**.

### 4. Industrial Horror & Unprocessed Battlefield Weight
Witty's identification of the titan crystal as **artificial** turned Ellara's Session 8 screaming-lamp nightmare into cold reality—an *"abomination"* fueled by living people. Layered behind that sits the unresolved weight of helping lure roughly **80 Ducal soldiers** into Hýrda's collapsed mine (even if it remains unconfirmed whether any are still trapped alive inside).

### 5. Torn Between Pharus, [[Arkos]], and Staying
Hýrda woke sharp homesickness for [[Arkos]] (*"All the colors"*), and her Seminary room held **no replies from [[Lyra Meadowlight]]** to her Session 3 and Session 10 letters—only Eduard's Pharus envelope. Pharus promises safety and a far larger library for her historical questions. Choosing to return to Whitman Manor *"for now"* is an active moral decision not to leave her companions behind while another crystal is still being built.

### 6. The Split Between Radiant Magic and Hidden Druidic Roots
Ellara wields light- and star-coded magic (*Starry Form (Archer)*, *Moonbeam*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Faerie Fire*, *Light*, *Bless*) openly in battle and celebration, while continuing to hide her beast/nature magic—using spider Wild Shape in secret (escaping captivity unseen in Session 16), summoning her owl covertly, and slipping away alone to cast *Speak with Animals*. Back in Bastion, her [[Spirit]] answered her herbs again after going silent in Hýrda, though left visibly shaken. She also reached **Level 5** at the end of Hýrda's battle and has not yet unveiled any 3rd-level spells in play.
