## Ellara's Current Headspace & Emotional Faultlines

### 1. Reconciled with Ysidor — Carrying the Weight of His Trust
Ellara's biggest emotional knot from Session 21 unraveled in Session 22. She pulled **Ysidor** aside, apologized for dismissing **Meri** as *"hearsay,"* owned the mirror between Meri and **Proctor Eduard**, told him openly about visiting Eduard, and confessed she had secretly finished her Eulogian blessing over the titan crystal. Ysidor scooped her off the ground in forgiveness (*"If you trust Proctor Eduard like I trust Meri, I trust him too, because you're my friend and I trust you"*). That grace brought huge relief—and raises the moral stakes if Eduard ever proves unworthy of it.

### 2. Sharp Unease About Donnathan Reeves vs. Lingering Cracks with Eduard
- **On Donnathan Reeves:** Watching Reeves across the dinner table, Ellara's **`Insight Dirty 20`** cut clean through his polish. She read him as cocky, controlling, asserting rank over Witty in Witty's own home (dismissing the chef without checking with his host), and barely bothering to hide how little he thinks of Witty. His lavish **Pharus** envoy offer feels like a gilded way to manage or remove Witty rather than genuine mentorship.
- **On Proctor Eduard:** Eduard remained warm, composed through Berridin's leaf-costume chaos, protective when Witty tried to push Ellara into Fabergé's job or into traveling with him, and gracious in the carriage once Ellara earnestly cleared up his *"first crush"* teasing (for *"many, many reasons"*). Yet cracks remain:
  - At dinner, Eduard remarked there had been whispers that **Michelle Fabergé** was *"not so proper,"* explaining why the Eulogia is pressing for a *"proper Eulogian"* replacement—an unsettling phrase given Fabergé's private *"How much more blood will this cost"* note.
  - Ellara still has **never asked** Eduard her Session 18 questions about **Hýr**, the censored mountain-splitting name, or Eulogian scripture.
  - Her [[Spirit]] felt **unnerved, uncertain, shaken, and sad** the night after she first confided in Eduard, and Eduard's upcoming private meeting with Reeves later this week will be a major test of whether Eduard is truly kept in the dark.

### 3. Fewer Secrets Inside the Party, Still Guarded Toward Eduard
Ellara's honesty with Ysidor cleared her conscience with the party—both **Nyx** and **Ysidor** now know about her Session 21 talk with Eduard, and the whole group collaborated openly on bringing Eduard to dinner. Toward **Eduard**, however, she still withholds Nyx's role in killing **Michelle Fabergé**, her companions' true identities as the disguised staff, the Hýrda mine collapse, and the titan crystal sitting inside Whitman Manor.

### 4. Pharus Pulling from Both Sides — While Bastion Loose Ends Remain
Both **Proctor Eduard** (who confirmed [[Pharos|Pharus]] is the political seat of the [[Eulogia]] and renewed his study offer without pressure) and **Donnathan Reeves** (who offered Witty a first-class Lighthouse envoy post scouting **Torchlight College**) are now pointing straight toward the imperial capital. Pharus promises the Grand Library for her Hýr/Nites questions and keeps her alongside her friends—yet leaving Bastion too quickly risks losing track of **Darius**, the **Denmother**, **[[Lyra Meadowlight]]'s** missing replies, and what Eduard actually learns from Reeves later this week.

### 5. Industrial Horror & Unprocessed Battlefield Weight
Beneath the dinner-table politics, the artificial titan crystal (*"abomination"*) still sits at Whitman Manor, and the unresolved weight of helping lure roughly **80 Ducal soldiers** into Hýrda's collapsed mine (with their survival unconfirmed) remains quietly unprocessed.

### 6. The Split Between Radiant Magic and Hidden Druidic Roots
Ellara continues to present light- and star-coded magic (*Starry Form (Archer)*, *Moonbeam*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Faerie Fire*, *Light*, *Bless*) openly while keeping her beast/nature magic hidden (spider Wild Shape, covert owl, solo *Speak with Animals*). Her [[Spirit]] remained quiet through Session 22, and she still has not unveiled any **Level 5 (3rd-level)** Druid spells in play.
