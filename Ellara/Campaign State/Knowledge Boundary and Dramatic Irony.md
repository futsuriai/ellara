## 1. What Ellara Actually Knows In-Character (Through Session 22)

### Faith, History & Her Spirit
- **[[Arkos]] Backstory (`[[Ellara]]`, `[[Sunstone Quarry]]`, `[[Highland Standing Stones]]`, `[[Correspondence]]`):** Ellara knows she was raised in the Priory of the Steadfast Light after being orphaned, that her [[Spirit]] first manifested around [[Lyra Meadowlight]]'s mother's death, and that Light Crystals plus *"Standing Stones"* (labeled Age of Shadow artifacts) were unearthed deep in Arkos's [[Sunstone Quarry]] 8 years ago (643 AE)—a connection she has long feared might touch her own abilities.
- **Hýr, Nites & "The Static" (`dnd:session-17.md`–`session-19.md`):** She knows **Hýr** is real, slumbered ~600–650 years, and claims *it* split/moved itself at the plea of an unnamed young woman—contradicting Eulogian scripture on **Nites** (a name Hýr does not recognize). Vast, painful magic ("the static") redacts that woman's name even from Hýr. Meri also taught her that Nites is absent from pre-imperial Hýrda lore, and that the Empire hunted people labeled *"shadows"* alongside pre-kingdom ancient beings (leaving survivors hidden).
- **Her Spiritual Experiences (`session-2.md`, `4.md`, `8.md`, `14.md`, `17.md`, `21.md`):** She heard unreadable foreign syllables in a dream (S2), guiding reassurance with a warning before the Lighthouse (S4), screaming agony from Eduard's desk lamp (S8), total silence in Hýrda (S14), strange magical resonance near Hýr (S17), and a shaken, uncertain, sad [[Spirit]] when meditating with highland herbs back in Bastion (S21). *(Canon leaves open whether "her spirit" and "the voice" are the same entity.)*
- **What She Has NOT Yet Connected:** She has **not** yet tied Arkos's Standing Stones or her Spirit's origin to Hýr, Nites, the Static, or synthetic crystal production.

### The Conspiracy, Eduard & Bastion Politics
- **Light Crystals (`session-6.md`, `8.md`, `21.md`):** She knows natural crystals form from decaying living matter near ancient ruins, whereas Duskbreaker Lighthouse secretly manufactures stronger synthetic crystals by **consuming living Shadowed subjects**. She has seen the titan's head-sized **artificial** crystal (~100x ordinary output, catastrophic if overloaded, costing *"a lot"* of lives) on Whitman Manor's table.
- **Proctor Eduard Evidence (`session-2.md`, `3.md`, `7.md`, `8.md`, `9.md`, `12.md`, `14.md`, `21.md`, `22.md`):** She knows Eduard's name appeared on Stonewall Tower's ledger taking Meri into personal custody (S2), he claimed he released her (S3), Meri's S7 jail-priest description matched Eduard (S7—Ellara recognized him from the description, though Meri never knew his name), he is fifth son of the Marchioness of Marchenbau and grand-uncle to Empress Consort Lyssandra (S8), he publicly defended warforged as *"gifts of light"* (S9), he gifted *Josephine's Insights on the Nature of Eulogia* (S12), and Berridin *claimed* he overheard Eduard boast Ellara was *"wrapped around his finger"* (S13–14).
- **Eduard's S21–22 Account (`session-21.md:121–159, 173–175`; `session-22.md:124, 167, 173, 188, 196`):** She read sincere, fatherly concern in his S21 welcome on the Seminary grounds (`Insight 26`). In his office, he expressed shock, claimed he was told crystals came from ruins and the northern army was a territorial dispute, said **Donnathan Reeves** stalled his inquiries, noted the head researcher died with **foul play suspected**, and offered an undated, church-funded **rail writ to [[Pharos|Pharus]]**. In Session 22, he confirmed the church's political seat is in Pharus (even if mythos holds it was born in Bastion), stated the Eulogia wants a *"proper Eulogian"* to replace Fabergé amid whispers she was *"not so proper,"* arranged a private meeting later this week with Reeves (who promised to make time *"given recent incidences"*), and accepted Ellara's earnest clarification that Witty is strictly a friend.
- **Lighthouse vs. Duke Structure & Reeves's S22 Offer (`session-9.md`, `21.md`, `22.md`):** She knows Reeves and **Duke Valerian Oliander** are childhood best friends and romantic partners, that **Duskbreaker Lighthouse** is independent (Fabergé led research, Reeves runs relations), and that the Duke funds it without running it—leaving whether the Duke knows about human synthesis unconfirmed. In Session 22, she learned Fabergé's Director of Research post remains unfilled under scrutiny between the Grand Duke and the Eulogia, saw Reeves rule Witty out and instead offer him a temporary, funded **Lighthouse Envoy** post in **Pharus** to scout **Torchlight College** (signing tomorrow at the Lighthouse), and read Reeves directly (`Insight Dirty 20`) as cocky, controlling, asserting rank over Witty in Witty's own home, and thinking very little of Witty.
- **Darius & Fabergé (`session-5.md`, `6.md`, `20.md`, `21.md`):** She met artificer **Darius** outside Fabergé's workshop, discussed East-Side water purification and crystal concurrency, missed their Tuesday meetup at *The Wailing Pig*, secretly infiltrated Fabergé's workshop as a spider (reading *"How much more blood will this cost"* and taking *Dimension Door* rune sketches + spare), watched Darius panic and bolt to report Fabergé missing, and knows Nyx killed Fabergé the night before.
- **Party Truths:** She knows Nyx is a former paid assassin who is 600+ years old with ties to Hýr (`session-16.md`, `17.md`, `20.md`), trusts her as level-headed, and has experienced lifelong betrayals (`session-21.md`); Berridin grew up orphaned and shared Samantha as his first kill (`session-14.md`), and found enough camp gold to pay off Witty's estate loan (`session-20.md`); Ysidor is Meri's chosen heir, wants to visit [[Arkos]] (`session-17.md`, `18.md`), now knows about Ellara's S21 Eduard visit and secret crystal blessing, and forgave her wholeheartedly (`session-22.md`); Witty knows Donnathan Reeves through family ties and is set to sign Reeves's Pharus envoy contract tomorrow (`session-21.md`, `22.md`).

## 3. Canon Disambiguation & Repo Quirks

- **Duskbreaker vs. Dawnbreaker Lighthouse:** Canonical name is **Duskbreaker Lighthouse** (`Dawnbreaker Lighthouse` in `dnd:session-19.md:148` is a one-off typo).
- **Hýrda vs. Hyrta:** Canonical display name is **Hýrda** (`hyrta` is a legacy entity ID in `dnd/src/store/locations.js`; `Hyrda` unaccented appears in S13/15/16).
- **Arkos vs. Arcos:** **Arkos** is canonical in this vault and `dnd/src/store/correspondence.js`; **Arcos** appears in `dnd:session-17.md`, `locations.js`, and `sessions.js`. Both refer to Ellara's highland hometown.
- **Pharus vs. Pharos:** Canonical city spelling in `dnd` stores and Session 21–22 notes is **Pharus**, while the existing vault page stem in `~/source/ellara` is `Pharos.md` (link as `[[Pharos|Pharus]]`).
- **Whitman vs. Whitaker:** Witty's full name is **Whitaker "Witty" Whitman VI** (`Whitaker` = shared given name of sons I–VI; `Whitman` = surname, matching **Whitman Manor** in `session-21.md`), though older `dnd` stores use `lord-whitakers-estate` and `whitaker-family`.
- **Two Separate Bounties on Meri:** Do **not** conflate the **250 gp Black Swan** underworld contract (`session-11.md`, `lore.js`) with the **10,000 gp** bounty cited by the Ducal lieutenant (`session-19.md:210`).
- **Character Level Drift:** The party reached **Level 5** at the end of Session 19 (`session-19.md:382`, `sessions.js`), though `[[Details]]` still shows stale **Level 3**.
- **Spirit vs. Bastion Voice Ambiguity:** Preserve canon ambiguity between her awake `[[Spirit]]` (`session-14.md:132`, `session-21.md:165–171`) and her sleeping *"whispers of Eulogia"* / *"that voice"* (`session-2.md:14`, `session-4.md:272`, `session-14.md:158`).
- **Insight Check Scopes:**
  - **S21 `Insight 26` (`session-21.md:121–123, 270`):** Applied **strictly** to Eduard's sincere, fatherly concern in his welcome **on the Seminary grounds**—not his later office shock, claims about ruins/Reeves, or Pharus offer.
  - **S22 `Insight Dirty 20` (`session-22.md:186`):** Applied **strictly** to **Donnathan Reeves's** demeanor/power dynamics at dinner (cocky, controlling, treating Witty's house as his own, dismissing Nyx without checking with Witty, barely hiding his low opinion of Witty)—not to Proctor Eduard.
