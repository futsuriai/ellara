# Session 21 Analysis — *The Lives Within the Light*

> Baseline catch-up brief integrating **Session 20 (*Victory, with Interest*)** and **Session 21 (*The Lives Within the Light*)** ahead of **Session 22 (*Change It from Within*)**.
> Living summary files: [[Ellara Current State]] · [[Ellara Threads and Callbacks]] · [[Knowledge Boundary and Dramatic Irony]].

## Snapshot at Session Close

- **Location & Time:** Morning of Day 3 back in [[Bastion City]], at **Whitman Manor** (Lord Whitaker's Estate).
- **Immediate Outcome:** Following Hýrda's victory (Session 19), the party extracted a head-sized crystal from the destroyed Warforged Titan, gathered recent Ducal orders/route map and recovered gold from the camp, rushed back to Bastion where Berridin paid off Witty's estate loan (`1,000 gp + 49 gp` fee), and analyzed the crystal at Whitman Manor.
- **Party Objective Locked In:** Witty confirmed the titan crystal is **artificial** (~100x ordinary crystal output, catastrophic if overloaded, forged from *"a lot"* of living Shadowed). Clarifying that **Duskbreaker Lighthouse** is independent of **Duke Valerian Oliander** (who funds rather than directs it), the party committed to stopping living-subject crystal production by having Witty apply for **Michelle Faberge's** vacant research post through his family's connection to **Donnathan Reeves**.
- **Ellara's Personal Position:** Chose to stay with the party *"for now"* while holding an **undated, church-funded rail writ to [[Pharos]]** from **Proctor Eduard** (`session-21.md:173–177`).

---

## What Ellara Is Thinking (Headspace)

- **Relieved by Eduard, yet unable to silence her gut:** Her `Insight 26` read sincere, fatherly concern in Eduard's welcome on the Seminary grounds, and his office shock + blame on Reeves gave her deep relief (*"I knew it. I knew I could trust you"*). Yet she called Meri's warning *"hearsay"* despite having matched Eduard's description in Session 7 herself, left all her Session 18 Hýr/Nites questions unasked, and later found her own [[Spirit]] unnerved, uncertain, shaken, and sad.
- **Guilty over secrecy in both directions:** Kept Faberge's killer, party identities, the cave-in, and the titan crystal hidden from Eduard (flickering her eyes down at his mention of suspected foul play); kept her visit to Eduard hidden from Ysidor, Witty, and Berridin (confiding only in Nyx); and kept her `Deception 17` secret blessing over the crystal hidden from Ysidor.
- **Torn by Ysidor's rebuke, steadied by Nyx's empathy:** Ysidor's rejection of Eulogian last rites for Shadowed dead (*"they didn't feel welcome when they were alive. I don't think they'll feel very welcome when they're dead"*) shook her *"The light is for everybody"* conviction, while Nyx quietly backed her up over coffee, shared his own history of betrayals, and reminded her that another crystal is coming if they walk away.
