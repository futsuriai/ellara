## What Ellara Is Thinking (Headspace)

- **Deep relief and restored trust with Ysidor:** Confessing both her visit to Eduard and her secret crystal blessing—and having Ysidor sweep her off her feet in forgiveness (*"If you trust Proctor Eduard like I trust Meri, I trust him too, because you're my friend and I trust you"*)—lifted the heaviest emotional bruise from Session 21. Seeing the mirror between Meri and Eduard gave her genuine clarity.
- **Sharp unease about Donnathan Reeves:** Her `Insight Dirty 20` cut cleanly through Reeves's polished charm. She saw a cocky, controlling man who treated Witty's home as his own, dismissed the chef without glancing at his host, and barely hid how little he thought of Witty. Being sent to **Pharus** on Reeves's dime feels less like generosity and more like a convenient leash or relocation.
- **Quietly unsettled by Eduard's "proper Eulogian" remark:** On the surface, Eduard was protective, composed through the leaf-costume chaos, arranged a private talk later this week with Reeves (who promised to make time *"given recent incidences"*), and respected her earnest boundary about Witty in the carriage. Yet his comment about whispers that **Michelle Fabergé** was *"not so proper"*—and the Eulogia insisting on a *"proper Eulogian"* to replace her—sits awkwardly beside what Ellara already knows about Fabergé's *"How much more blood will this cost"* note and the Lighthouse's basement horrors.
- **Pharus pulling from two directions at once:** Now both Proctor Eduard (through her church-funded writ and Eulogian education at the church's political seat) and Donnathan Reeves (through Witty's funded Lighthouse envoy post scouting **Torchlight College**) are pointing straight toward [[Pharos|Pharus]]. Ellara wants to stay with her friends *and* uncover the truth, while worrying that leaving Bastion before answers surface—or letting Witty walk into Reeves's contract blindly—could play right into someone else's hands.
- **Mild lingering embarrassment—and gratitude for clarity:** Between Berridin winking and dropping a leaf, Witty abruptly nominating her for Fabergé's post and pitching her as his travel companion, and Eduard teasing her about a *"first crush,"* the evening was mortifying—but finally setting Eduard straight (for *"many, many reasons"*) felt freeing.
