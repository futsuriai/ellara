# Session 19 — *Operation Don’t Fuck It Up*

**Locales:** Hýrda · Imperial encampment · Mountain cave · Battlefield outside the mountain
**Time:** Pre-dawn after the party’s return → several days of preparation → army’s arrival before noon

---

## 1) The Mountain Remembers

The army was only days away, leaving Hýrda less than a week to prepare. After returning around two or three in the morning, the party took what rest they could. Witty and Nyx slept beneath the stars; the others stayed at Ysidor’s home. Ellara rose early for prayer, while Nyx prepared his crossbow bolts.

Ellara found Meri working around the village and asked to hear the mountain’s history. Meri agreed on one condition: Little Hill would attend as part of his training. Ellara collected Ysidor from his morning exercise—throwing stones, retrieving them, and throwing them again—and assured him he was not in trouble.

**Ysidor:** "I guess. I like physical more than emotional training."

In a makeshift training arena, Meri laid out the history Ysidor had neglected.

Long before the Empire or the Great Cataclysm, stone goliaths lived throughout the world’s mountain ranges. Their oldest accounts described devastating storms that drove many communities deeper into the mountains; some disappeared and were never heard from again. When the survivors emerged, a new kingdom—what Meri called a “wannabe empire”—was gathering power.

Over the following centuries, that Empire began naming people “shadows” and hunting them. Many goliaths fled east, eventually reaching this mountain and finding kin already sheltered here. Those predecessors taught them of **Hýr**, a great being who was either the spirit of the mountain or the mountain itself, and of a faith grounded in love and acceptance.

Hýr was not the only ancient being. Stories spoke of a tree older than the kingdom and a great beast in the seas that sang lonesomely. Some such beings had been worshipped as gods; others were simply creatures of unimaginable age. Most had since been hunted and killed. Any survivors were likely hidden beyond the Eulogia’s reach.

Hýr’s refuge endured because the Empire needed only the mountain’s existing passage for its cities and rails. It never carved deeply enough to discover everything within. As imperial expansion displaced more goliaths, Hýrda slowly grew. Other communities might still survive in distant mountains—or even elsewhere within Hýr herself—but Meri either could not or would not say.

This was not a magical fireside story. It was the history of a people driven from their homes, and a warning about the Empire that had driven them.

---

## 2) Faith, Light, and Silence

Ellara’s excitement sharpened into questions. In none of the old stories, she observed, did **Nites** appear.

Nites had entered Hýrda’s history only as far as the Empire had. The villagers learned of the Eulogia later and had otherwise kept largely to themselves for centuries. Even Hýr rarely spoke. She sometimes answered prayers, and her chosen clerics could heal, but hearing her voice directly within one’s mind—as the party had when she spoke of moving herself—was extraordinary.

Meri asked whether Ellara had ever heard the Eulogian Seminary or the Eternal Light speak to her.

**Ellara:** "I hope so."

**Meri:** "You hope so? I suppose that’s faith, isn’t it?"

**Ellara:** "Yes, I suppose that is faith."

Meri reminded her that many people claimed to hear the Eternal Light. One such priest had helped imprison Meri. If that priest truly had been guided by the Light, would Ellara be obliged to repeat his actions?

Ellara rejected the premise almost indignantly. The Light could not have commanded such cruelty; the priest must have been thwarted by the Shadow Monarch.

**Meri:** "I would be careful of faith. Faith can keep you strong and be a shelter. But faith can also..."

**Ellara:** "That is true, but I believe without faith, we are just—we are not alive."

Meri called it a hard belief for someone so young, though not necessarily a wrong one. Everyone placed faith somewhere: in the Eternal Light, in Hýr, or simply in the kindness of strangers.

Then Meri turned to Ysidor and asked about his swordsmanship. He had plainly absorbed very little of the lesson.

**Ellara:** "I believe he’ll do his best."

Meri immediately sparred with him and thoroughly proved how much training Little Hill still needed.

## 3) Preparations, Reconnaissance, and a Missing Fortune

Berridin proposed observing the cave entrance each day from a distance, and Nyx accompanied him. The imperial camp was still recovering from the party’s sabotage and induced sickness. Soldiers remained pale but were moving again. Guard duty around the cave was light and rotated between one or two people, supplemented by the camp’s watchtower. The main force had not yet arrived, and there were no unusual troop movements.

Witty, meanwhile, explored the possibilities of his artificer elixirs. His unused Boldness and Resilience elixirs—and their vials—vanished when the long rest ended. He planned to replace them with one healing elixir and one flight elixir, the latter granting a flying speed of ten feet for ten minutes.

With his hair tied into a bun and a little Whitaker basket over his arm, Witty wandered into the surrounding land singing and whistling. As **Whitaker “Witty” Whitman VI**, he had received little attention from his parents and spent much of his childhood roaming the family estate, where the woodsmen befriended him and taught him about plants.

The common ingredients he needed were easy to find. More interesting were mushrooms resembling those used to sicken the soldiers. These instead numbed the body and produced a mild high. Witty recognized their potential (**Nature 17**) and gathered them, intending to concentrate them into a more potent gas in a makeshift laboratory inside Ysidor’s house.

Berridin undertook a more dangerous preparation alone: recovering the party’s confiscated gold.

He approached the encampment from the south, near the mess hall and Ellara’s former escape route, then cast **Invisibility**. Inside a larger, better-appointed tent, he found the captain who had struck him down seated behind a desk covered in papers and missives. The party’s familiar heavy purse—holding more than a thousand gold—hung from the captain’s person.

Berridin nearly abandoned the attempt before revealing one more secret: a tiny ooze familiar camouflaged against his body. The creature aided his illusions but, traumatized and painfully shy, refused to leave his side.

With the ooze’s help, Berridin slipped the purse away unnoticed (**Sleight of Hand with advantage 18**).

**Berridin:** "This is a very evil spell, thank you."

He walked safely out of camp, told no one what he had done, and presented the excursion as ordinary reconnaissance.

## 4) The Perfectly Concealed Last Stand

Ysidor helped the villagers raise wooden spikes and palisades. Witty assisted with the engineering and proposed constructing a ballista—or perhaps a trebuchet—capable of launching Ysidor into the approaching army.

**Witty:** "But think about it. A trebuchet launches rocks and stones."

Meri reminded him that Hýrda’s plan was to lure the army into the mountain and collapse the passage, not fight a fortified battle.

**Meri:** "Sometimes faith is a good thing. And when the Goddess of the mountain tells you she’s gonna help you, I’m gonna bet on that. Instead of throwing Little Hill at the cave entrance."

She considered Ysidor.

**Meri:** "He’s already a few pebbles short of a hill at times. Don’t want to shake it up anymore."

Undeterred, Witty proposed a hidden pit filled with spikes. Ysidor immediately began digging directly in front of his own house.

**Witty:** "We might have to defend Ysidor’s house at some point. This might be the last stand."

They concealed the completed trap so flawlessly that the earth appeared untouched (**Witty Deception with advantage: Nat 20**). They also neglected to warn anyone.

Berridin returned first, triumphant and carrying the recovered fortune. He failed to notice the trap, felt the ground soften beneath him, and stepped into empty air. His attempt to catch himself failed (**Dexterity save vs. DC 15: failure**), and he landed on the spikes for **15 damage**. The gold purse remained intact but fell inches beyond his reach.

Nyx arrived to find Berridin bloodied and impaled while Ysidor admired the successful design.

**Nyx:** "How the fuck were you gonna get that bag all impaled like that? Seriously?"

**Ysidor:** "This is your fault."

**Nyx:** "You walked into a spike trap that one of us made—your own team, on home turf. That’s on you."

Ysidor then approached carrying logs and gear.

**Ysidor:** "Oh, you guys found the spike trap?"

Nyx pulled Berridin free and fished out the gold with a large stick. Ysidor restored **20 HP** with **Lay on Hands**. Witty attempted to provide additional healing but handed Berridin the flight elixir instead, leaving him floating above the pit for ten minutes.

Ellara arrived to find Berridin hovering with his legs spread and fingers waggling in Nyx’s face.

**Nyx:** "Don’t ask. Just—it’s as bad as it looks."

**Ellara:** "Perhaps I will continue my prayers elsewhere for now."

Meri soon discovered the pit and ordered Ysidor to dismantle it. Once she left, Witty and Ysidor secretly reset it. They placed a conspicuous ring of potted plants around the hidden danger to protect villagers, planning to remove the warning if the army reached Hýrda.

## 5) The Plan at the Cave

That evening, scouts reported that the army would arrive before noon the following day. Meri gathered the party for a final meeting.

Berridin would disguise himself as Meri and bait the army into the cave. Once most of the force was inside, he would escape and trigger the explosives. The mechanism had a large button inside the cave and a backup plunger outside.

Berridin initially intended to turn invisible and slip back through the entire pursuing force.

**Berridin:** "That’s a serious flaw."

Instead, Witty would prepare another flight elixir after the next rest. Berridin planned to combine flight and invisibility, though the elixir’s ten-foot speed and the cave’s limited overhead clearance left little margin for error.

Nyx camouflaged himself for long-range support near the camp. Witty assembled a rough ghillie suit from foliage and purple flowers, then hid near the cave mouth and backup plunger, ready to repair the mechanism if it failed. Ellara took a relatively protected position roughly ninety feet from the expected battle.

By morning, the approaching army could be heard rumbling in the distance.

Roughly one hundred units arrived: more than eighty soldiers, several smaller modern warforged, and a colossal warforged titan carrying riders on platforms built across its body. The machine resembled the construct previously seen at Dawnbreaker Lighthouse and stopped outside the camp.

**Ellara:** "Oh boy."

**Ysidor:** "Oh, we can take him."

The defenders were badly outnumbered. Everything depended on getting most of the army underground.

From his sniper’s nest, Nyx watched the newly arrived commander receive the camp’s captain.

*We can’t fight these people. They have to go in—as many as possible.*

He checked the sending stones.

**Nyx:** "Is everybody ready?"

**Ysidor:** "Ready."

**Nyx:** "Operation Don’t Fuck It Up is a go."

**Berridin:** "I think this is it. I’m ready."

After a beat:

**Berridin:** "This is a normal plan."

Ellara cast **Bless** on Berridin, Nyx, and Ysidor.

---

## 6) A Bounty Worth Chasing

Wearing Meri’s form, Berridin approached from the cave side of the encampment and remained beyond effective bow range. The cave’s direct guard had left to help receive the army, though the watchtower remained occupied.

Berridin broadcast his challenge through the sending stone.

**Berridin as Meri:** "We’ve had enough of your... Come face me. We defend with our very lives. I know that I’ve been your prize this entire time. Me and my people will fight, and you will soon regret your actions here."

The guards were openly incredulous that the cave-dwellers wanted an imperial army to pursue them underground. After they conferred, a stately red-haired lieutenant in full uniform stepped forward. She was exhausted and irritated after a month-long march.

**Lieutenant:** "I assume you’re Meri of Hýrda."

**Berridin as Meri:** "Your homeland is the might of the mountains."

**Lieutenant:** "These lands do not belong to you, but to the Duke, and are the rightful property of the Empire."

She initially ordered only a small regiment and two warforged forward. Berridin demanded more.

**Berridin as Meri:** "You will be crushed if you don’t send them all. We can take them all."

Ysidor stepped out to support the provocation.

**Ysidor:** "I told you they would be too chicken to actually confront us in the caves."

Ellara privately suggested framing the taunts as reverse psychology: “Meri” needed the army kept outside long enough for the villagers to destroy its resources.

**Berridin as Meri:** "Quiet, Little Hill. You’re supposed to be leading our camps as we distract them in the cave."

**Ysidor:** "I am? Am I supposed to be leading a force?"

The lieutenant stared at the confused performance as if regretting every decision that had led to her promotion. A **Berridin Deception check was requested**, though its result was not recorded.

Then the lieutenant mentioned the **10,000-gold bounty** on Meri. The surrounding soldiers took notice.

**Ysidor:** "Yeah, there’s so much bounty on Meri. I’m sure it’d be hard to split all that money once you kill her."

That was enough. The soldiers perked up, and the lieutenant ordered an immediate march.

Berridin and Ysidor retreated toward the cave under watchtower fire, the arrows falling short behind them. Roughly three-quarters of the army followed. The titan remained outside, too large to fit through the passage.

---

## 7) Eighty Soldiers Below, One Titan Above

Berridin and Ysidor ran deep into the cave with approximately eighty soldiers behind them. Witty watched the force pass from his hiding place near the entrance.

Outside, Meri and Hýrda’s defenders met the remaining soldiers, including the lieutenant’s contingent. The titan lumbered south to join that battle.

**Meri:** "Take care of that. We got the people."

Ellara and Witty entered the fight (**Initiative: Ellara 15; Witty 6**).

Ellara’s first attack struck the titan’s armor with a boom but caused no visible damage (**attack 12, miss**). Nyx closed to thirty feet and marked the construct with **Hunter’s Mark**. His first bolt found a gap in its armor (**Nat 20, 28 to hit**), carrying a critical **Dreadful Strike** for **11 additional damage**. His second shot glanced harmlessly away (**10, miss**).

The titan turned on him.

Nyx failed to withstand its hammer blow (**Strength save 8**) and was knocked prone. Its axe hand descended with advantage, dealing **25 damage** and knocking him unconscious.

Inside the cave, the pursuing soldiers packed in behind Berridin and Ysidor.

**Berridin:** "Get ready!"

Berridin tried to judge whether the full force had entered, but could not (**Intelligence 2**). Rather than risk detonating too early, he ran deeper and waited.

From his concealed post, Witty could see the last trails of soldiers entering the passage, the villagers fighting outside, Meri confronting the lieutenant, and the party struggling against the titan farther south. Panic set in, but he refused to abandon the trigger.

**Witty:** "I need to be here to blow the entrance."

If Berridin could not escape, Witty was prepared to seal the cave anyway.

**Witty:** "I’m ready to be the dead man switch."

---

## 8) Flight, Grease, and Repeated Resurrections

Ysidor finally grasped that he had willingly followed Berridin into a confined space containing roughly eighty imperial soldiers. Their solution was characteristically direct: Ysidor would drink the flight elixir, Berridin would turn him invisible, and Berridin would ride him out “magic carpet style.”

**Berridin:** "It’ll be perfect. It’s amazing."

Outside, Ellara moved within sixty feet of Nyx and restored him with **Healing Word for 9 HP**.

**Ellara:** "I’ll come back."

Her **Guiding Bolt** missed (**9**). Nyx healed himself for **13 HP**, then used **Blessing of the Raven Queen** to teleport thirty feet away without provoking an attack.

**Nyx:** "I’m back in the fight."

There was no cover in the open field. The titan followed, struck Nyx for **22 damage**, and knocked him unconscious again before turning toward Ellara.

**Nyx:** "Because I’m suicidal, that’s why."

Inside the cave, Berridin judged that most of the pursuing army had finally entered. He handed Ysidor the flight elixir, turned him invisible, and climbed onto his back. With only ten feet of flying movement—or twenty feet while dashing—their escape would take several turns.

Witty abandoned the detonator post to help the others. He cast **Grease** across the battlefield, took a running start, and successfully turned himself into a rancid projectile (**Intelligence 22**). Soldiers slipped as he slid feet-first through the battle for roughly forty feet, clad in flowers and foliage and coated in pork-rind grease.

**Witty:** "I’m doing it like Tom Cruise in that movie."

**Nyx:** "Nice and rancid. Good."

Ellara restored Nyx for another **9 HP**, but her next attack missed (**16**). Nyx refused to disengage; if the titan rejoined the army, Hýrda would be overrun. His first bolt missed, but his second struck with a **21** and carried another **Dreadful Strike**. The titan retaliated for **18 damage**, knocking him unconscious for a third time.

Witty’s **Ray of Sickness** missed, but his **Healing Word restored Nyx for 6 HP**. He then contacted Berridin, assuring him it was acceptable to kill everything trapped in the mountain.

**Witty:** "Just making sure you’re okay with it."

---

## 9) Bring Down the Mountain

Invisible Ysidor carried Berridin over the soldiers until, after roughly sixty feet, they reached the detonator.

**Ysidor:** "Let’s go, let’s blow it up."

He pressed the button. The mechanism began its sequence.

Outside, Ellara brought **Moonbeam** down on the titan. It failed its save (**Constitution save 3 vs. DC 14**) and took **12 radiant damage** as pure daylight poured over its armor. Ellara entered her glowing **Starry Form**, taking the shape of the Archer.

Nyx saw the construct burning and Ellara shining in the corner of his vision.

*Fuck my health.*

His first crossbow shot struck critically for **15 damage**; the second missed. He retreated, narrowly avoiding the titan’s opportunity attack, and the construct followed him out of the Moonbeam. Both of its next attacks missed. After being knocked unconscious so many times, Nyx’s thinking had simplified: either he killed the titan, or it killed him.

Witty, out of spell slots, hurled **Acid Splash**. The titan catastrophically failed to avoid it (**Dexterity save Nat 1**) and took a single point of acid damage.

At last, Ysidor and Berridin reached the cave mouth. They took the longer, safer route around the encampment.

The explosives detonated behind them.

A tremendous boom rolled across the battlefield. The ground shook, and the mountain passage collapsed over the army trapped within.

---

## 10) The Titan’s Last Stand

With the cave sealed, the party concentrated on the titan.

Ellara moved her Moonbeam back over it. The construct resisted with a **natural 20**, but still took **9 radiant damage**. Nyx checked the stones from six hit points.

**Nyx:** "Is everybody okay?"

**Ysidor:** "All good on my side."

**Ellara:** "Yeah, we made it, somehow."

**Berridin:** "Yeah, it’s awesome. I’ve been flying."

Nyx warned them he was returning to the titan, fired twice, and missed with a **9 and 13**. He used his final **Blessing of the Raven Queen** to teleport away.

The titan turned on Witty, the last person to damage it. Its first blow hit with a **24** for **19 damage** and knocked him prone after he failed his Strength save (**14 vs. DC 17**). Its second attack hit with a **25** for **28 damage**, leaving Witty unconscious and only three damage short of instant death.

**Nyx:** "This is why splitting up the team is such a bad idea."

Witty’s first death save succeeded (**15**).

Ysidor dashed toward the fight with Berridin still on his back. From thirty feet away, Berridin restored Witty with **Healing Word for 9 HP** and created a **Minor Illusion** of Nyx beside the titan. Ellara’s Guiding Bolt failed spectacularly (**Nat 1**), and her Starry Form arrow also missed. Nyx’s shots followed with an **11 and 12**, both misses.

The titan studied the false Nyx and failed to recognize the illusion (**Investigation 11**). It wasted one attack striking the image, then turned and knocked Witty unconscious again.

**Witty:** "I’m dead. Again."

Berridin’s **Vicious Mockery** pierced the construct’s mind (**Wisdom save 4 vs. DC 13**), dealing **2 psychic damage** and imposing disadvantage on its next attack. After being restored, Witty fired his shortbow at close range and missed with a **5**.

**Ellara:** "Welcome to the club."

Witty tried to flee. Even with disadvantage, the titan’s opportunity attack was a dirty **20**, striking him in the back of the head for **20 damage**.

**Witty:** "All right. I should have stayed still."

Ysidor finally reached the titan and drove his war pick into it for **17 damage**. With the machine visibly failing, he cast **Shield of Faith** and placed himself between it and the others.

Ellara returned Moonbeam to the titan. It failed its save and burned. Nyx moved into range; his first shot missed, but his second hit with a **21 for maximum damage**. The titan remained standing and attacked Ysidor twice, but both blows missed with **12s**.

Berridin restored Witty again and conjured another illusion of Nyx. Witty’s next **Acid Splash** produced an exact repeat of the first: the titan rolled another **natural 1**.

This time Witty refused to risk moving.

**Witty:** "This thing’s a robot, right? I’m going to Jurassic Park it."

He sat motionless in his filthy, flowered ghillie suit and pretended to be a bush. Ysidor moved beside him and applied **Lay on Hands for 10 HP**.

**Witty:** "Wait, but I’m hiding as a bush. Don’t give me away."

**Ellara:** "He’s healing the bush."

---

## 11) The Final Bolt

Ellara kept the Moonbeam focused on the titan. It resisted with a dirty **20**, but still took **5 radiant damage** and began moving erratically, sparks tearing from its frame.

Then Ellara’s Starry Form arrow finally struck (**23 to hit**) for **6 damage**. The titan stood on the edge of collapse.

Nyx raised both crossbows. His first shot missed with a **13**.

The second hit with a **25**.

Nyx drew his off-hand crossbow and fired directly through the titan’s eye. The massive warforged toppled backward, struck the ground, and lay still.

He thought there should be a one-liner, but he was too exhausted to find one.

With the titan destroyed and the mountain passage collapsed, most of the surviving Ducal Army surrendered while Hýrda’s defenders finished the battle.

The party advanced to **level 5**.
