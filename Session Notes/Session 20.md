# Session 20 — *Victory, with Interest*

**Locales:** Battlefield south of Hýrda · Abandoned ducal encampment · Hýrda · Mountain road · Bastion East Gate · Bastion bank · Nyx’s townhouse · Lord Whitaker’s Estate
**Time:** Aftermath of the battle → celebratory night → following dawn → five days into the return journey

---

## 1) The Heart of the Machine

The mountain passage had collapsed over most of the Ducal Army, Hýrda’s defenders had broken the survivors, and the colossal warforged lay destroyed south of the shattered force. With the immediate battle over, the party turned to what remained.

Berridin examined the fallen construct but made little sense of its ruined mechanisms (**Investigation 4**). Witty joined him and promptly disappeared into the work (**Investigation 15 + 7 = 22**). As an artificer, he could follow the machine’s wires and appreciate the intricacies of its construction in a way the others could not.

Deep inside the warforged, he found an enormous **light crystal**. The crystals the party had previously encountered were roughly fist-sized; this one was as large as a person’s head. Witty worked it loose with a screwdriver, then casually threw it toward Berridin.

Berridin caught the unexpectedly heavy crystal (**Dexterity 17 + 4 = 21**). It felt far more solid than he expected, as though it would not crack easily despite its familiar crystalline appearance.

Witty studied what they had recovered (**History 26**). This was cutting-edge technology from the **Duskbreaker Lighthouse**, and selling it through conventional channels would be extraordinarily illegal. More importantly, the crystal was a powerful and dangerous energy source—strong enough to operate the massive warforged.

The possibilities were difficult to ignore. It might power **Michelle Faberge’s unfinished workshop device**, or perhaps even an airship. For Witty, the crystal also represented a possible answer to a more immediate fear: finding something valuable enough to repay the loan and protect his family’s estate.

## 2) Gold, Orders, and a Cooling Pot

Nyx scouted the abandoned ducal camp alone. He slipped inside without a sound (**Stealth 27**) and found no one waiting for them (**Perception 20**).

**Nyx:** "Looks like the coast is clear, but I don’t see anyone."

**Berridin:** "Let’s tear this place up."

The captain’s tent held a simple desk, rolled scrolls, and a worn jacket hanging from a chair. It was neither especially neat nor truly disorganized.

Nyx wanted the room searched immediately, but Berridin was still burdened by the massive crystal. Berridin began casting **Phantom Steed** outside so they could load it onto the summoned mount. Nyx, increasingly frustrated by the delay, interrupted him and took the crystal himself. Ellara noticed how strangely reluctant Berridin seemed to search for the money.

**Ellara:** "Master Din, are you feeling well?"

**Berridin:** "I am not okay."

At last, Berridin handed the crystal to Ysidor and began opening drawers. The search turned up recent orders, plans, and correspondence, though little that revealed more than the army’s latest movements (**Nyx Investigation 9; Berridin Investigation 5; Witty Investigation 14**).

It also produced money—something Berridin attempted to conceal.

His first effort held up well (**Berridin Deception: dirty 20; Witty Insight 11; Nyx Insight 14; Ellara Insight 20**), but Ellara continued pressing.

**Ellara:** "Master Din, are you sure you don’t know where the gold is?"

Berridin flipped a coin through his fingers.

**Berridin:** "You know, when I was searching last time, I found *a* gold pile, but it was a different gold pile, unfortunately."

The second lie fared worse (**Berridin Deception 15; Ysidor Insight 1; Witty Insight 7; Nyx Insight 14; Ellara Insight 16**). Witty began lamenting what would happen to his family.

**Ellara:** "Din. Please."

Berridin finally admitted that, “out of the goodness of my heart,” he had stumbled across a large treasure hoard. He assured Witty that the loan was covered and that there would still be money for the hot-air balloon, flicking coins toward Witty and Ysidor.

Nyx did not entirely understand the deception, but he understood that Berridin was engaged in some kind of money-related scheme. His distrust sharpened.

**Berridin:** "I know the combination to your safe."

**Nyx:** "I have two crossbows. Din, seriously—did you take anything from my apartment?"

**Berridin:** "No."

**Nyx:** "All right."

Nyx did not sound convinced.

The remainder of the camp offered little of value. Berridin found only ordinary tables, chairs, and military supplies (**Perception 10**). Nyx recovered a vague map of the army’s route (**Perception 9**), but the region was poorly charted by both the Empire and the Duke. Witty’s examination of the captain’s correspondence revealed similarly recent plans and movements (**Investigation 11**), but no great strategic archive.

In the mess hall, Berridin found a pot of cooling soup and helped himself. He survived the meal magnificently (**Constitution save: Nat 20**). It tasted as though it ought to be spicy, but was not.

Witty tried it next (**Constitution save 13**) and immediately projectile-vomited. The pot may have been a kind of continuously simmering “forever soup”—and possibly the same food the party had poisoned during their earlier sabotage.

**Berridin:** "Humans can’t handle spicy."

**Nyx:** "Witty, how are you doing? You know we poisoned that, right?"

**Witty:** "With what?"

**Nyx:** "Remember what you poisoned before you eat."

## 3) Everything Returns to Hyr

Meri approached the camp after seeing one of the party rush outside. She confirmed that Hýrda’s defenders had handled the remaining ducal soldiers. The future remained uncertain, and the village had suffered casualties, but those who died had understood the sacrifice they were making.

**Meri:** "Everything will return to Hyr."

She asked whether the party intended to remain in Hýrda. Ysidor admitted he had not thought much further than reaching home and defending it.

**Meri:** "One day you’ll be leading and need to think ahead."

**Ysidor:** "Yes, one day, but not for a long time."

**Meri:** "You think I’ll be around forever? Here I thought I was going to retire soon."

**Ysidor:** "We don’t retire. We just get old and die."

Berridin argued that the party should return to Bastion, but Meri insisted that they first share a celebratory supper—or, at minimum, some medicinal tea.

Ysidor placed a hand on Witty’s shoulder. Reasoning that poison was green and therefore its opposite ought to be purple, he made his hand glow purple and used his magic to purge the poison. With Witty recovering, Berridin pointed toward Hýrda.

**Berridin:** "Let’s go party!"

Ellara asked what a celebratory supper involved. Meri explained that the village simply shared whatever food and liquor it had. It was not so different from celebrations in Ellara’s own home village, though life in Bastion had made such customs feel distant.

First, however, the wounded needed bandaging and the dead needed care.

---

## 4) Michelle Faberge and the Shape of What Comes Next

Before the celebration, Nyx gathered the party to confront the question victory had left behind.

**Nyx:** "Okay, so what’s our next step? We stopped this battle, but now what?"

Witty suggested bringing the warforged’s power crystal to his idol, **Michelle Faberge**, to see what she could do with it. Ysidor agreed.

Nyx hesitated, then told them the truth.

**Nyx:** "Michelle Faberge is not with us anymore. There was an incident. She is not alive. She passed."

At Berridin’s prompting, Nyx explained that he had followed Michelle because the party needed information to infiltrate the Lighthouse. She caught him, a confrontation followed, and both of them fought.

**Nyx:** "I’m here, and she’s not. She was a worthy adversary. A worthy opponent."

Witty summarized the story more harshly: it sounded as though Nyx had stalked Michelle with the intention of torturing her for information and then murdered her.

Then, after a beat, he asked the question that genuinely stunned Nyx.

**Witty:** "Are they hiring for her position?"

**Nyx:** "Uh... are they hiring for her position? I assume so? I guess? Maybe?"

**Witty:** "Mm. I should apply someday."

Nyx told Witty that Michelle’s death had not been his intention, but the confrontation had become a choice between her life and his. He described it as part of his profession and admitted openly that he had killed for payment in the past, though no one had paid him for Michelle.

The confession returned them to the larger uncertainty. They had achieved an enormous objective, but people had died and none of them knew what the party was supposed to become afterward.

Witty’s immediate priority was staying with Berridin until they reached a bank and made the loan payment. Berridin insisted the debt was covered and urged everyone to return to Bastion.

Ellara believed they needed **Proctor Eduard’s help** to stop whatever the Lighthouse was doing with light crystals. Nyx warned her that he no longer knew whether Eduard would help them at all.

**Nyx:** "Keep your wits about you. If I were him, I could do whatever I want and you’d take it as truth."

Ellara shook her head as though Nyx understood nothing.

Nyx was exhausted in more ways than one. He thought about how unlike his old life this had become, whether he wanted to continue, and how little the victory had truly resolved. The immediate threat was broken, but the party had barely scratched the surface of something much larger.

For the moment, Witty wanted to check whether his house was still his. Nyx wanted sleep.

Berridin wanted to party.

## 5) A Hýrda Celebration

The village’s beer was far stronger than the party expected.

Ellara drank as though it were the weaker beer from her home village. Berridin tried to match her drink for drink. Witty finished early, returned for more, and eventually passed out without his trousers, his nightclothes in complete disarray.

Ysidor, accustomed to Hýrda’s drink, watched the others with deep amusement.

Berridin joined the stone goliaths and used **Prestidigitation** to create fireworks. As the night continued, the displays became progressively less precise. He followed them with **Major Illusion**, performing embellished recreations of his exploits (**Performance 18**). The villagers found him entertaining, though they believed his stories about as much as they would believe a drunk child. Ysidor acted as his enthusiastic accomplice while Ellara added **Faerie Fire**, bathing the celebration in colored light.

Nyx sat apart, having been ready to sleep since before the celebration began.

At some point, Ysidor left the revelry and went to the bodies. His duty to the village was not finished. He knelt before each of the dead, touched them with his religious symbol, and performed a simple rite.

**Ysidor:** "May Hýr guide you to the afterlife."

His blessing protected each body from becoming undead for seven days, except through the power of a **Wish** spell.

The village’s celebrations were physically affectionate and far more liberal than Ellara was accustomed to. She retained control of herself despite the drink (**Constitution save 20**), but remained somewhat scandalized by the villagers’ behavior.

Before dawn, Ellara asked Ysidor whether she could offer rites of her own. He welcomed every blessing the dead could receive.

As the first light rose, Ellara prayed over them.

**Ellara:** "I entrust your souls to the Light. May it shine for you evermore."

## 6) Little Hill Leaves Again

Morning found Witty in terrible condition: his nightcap crooked, his buttons misaligned, and an unexplained limp complicating his walk. Berridin was profoundly hungover. Ysidor was fine.

Ellara greeted them all with painful cheer.

As the party packed, Meri came to see them off. Ysidor asked one last time whether he ought to stay.

**Meri:** "We’ll survive. We’ll be fine. Don’t worry about us."

Ysidor reminded her about their sending stones—the little version of him in hers, and of her in his.

**Meri:** "So that’s how it works. Did they put some of your soul in there?"

**Ysidor:** "I don’t know, but I think my soul is still complete."

Meri turned to the others.

**Meri:** "Please take care of our Little Hill. He can be as dense as stone, but his heart is as big as a mountain."

**Nyx:** "And he’s as broad as a mountain."

**Meri:** "Are you hitting on my son?"

**Nyx:** "No, I’m giving him a compliment. I’ll take care of him."

Berridin asked whether the Duke might attack again. Meri believed it would take time to muster another force after losing an army. From Bastion, the party might learn of such an attempt sooner than Hýrda could.

Berridin promised they would return to rescue the village again, then unsuccessfully tried to secure more beer for the journey.

With that, the party began the road back to Bastion.

## 7) A Lesson in Interest

In the carriage, Witty reassured himself that everything would be fine. They had not spent any of the loan; they could simply return it.

Nyx was alarmed that someone raised with money knew so little about debt.

**Nyx:** "Witty, do you know what interest is?"

**Witty:** "Interest? I have lots of interests. Chemistry and math."

Nyx explained that interest was an additional percentage charged on top of borrowed money. Berridin vaguely remembered that the contract had included payment terms and something called “co-lateral.”

When Nyx asked for the contract, Berridin was surprised to learn he had been expected to keep it. All he remembered was something about a month passing and Witty’s estate.

Nyx grimaced.

Berridin tried to soften him with another coin. Nyx gave it back and repeated that a borrower had to repay what they received—and then more.

**Berridin:** "That seems extremely predatory. This is how the other side lives..."

When Berridin asked for the Eulogian position on lending, Ellara answered hesitantly that the Light regarded agreements between parties as holy. It was the responsibility of those involved to accept terms they could fulfill.

**Nyx:** "We’ll go back, we’ll pay it back, we’ll pay the interest, and then you’ll never get another loan. And if you do, you keep the paper."

**Berridin:** "What if you eat the paper? Is it still legal?"

The conversation dislodged a buried memory (**Berridin History 17**). Missing the first payment would make the entire debt due immediately—and allow the bank to seize the collateral immediately.

The collateral was Witty’s Bastion residence.

They appeared to have roughly five days before the deadline. Even at a good pace, the carriage was about seven days from the city.

The party debated continuous travel, changing drivers, and how much strain the horses could survive. Nyx had only just finished explaining the importance of honoring contracts.

Ultimately, Berridin and Ysidor decided to ride ahead using **Phantom Steed** and **Find Steed**, leaving the carriage behind.

The first day passed without a check. After that, the pace became punishing:

- On **day two**, both riders accumulated exhaustion but kept control of their mounts (**Animal Handling DC 10 with –2: Ysidor 15; Berridin 11**).
- On **day three**, the penalty worsened (**–4: Ysidor 8; Berridin 5**).
- On **day four**, Berridin succeeded through sheer fortune (**Nat 20 with –6**). Ysidor rolled a **0**, fell from his steed, and was left on the roadside while Berridin continued without noticing.
- On **day five**, Berridin pushed onward with four levels of exhaustion (**18 – 8 = 10**) and reached Bastion alone.

## 8) John Smith Settles the Account

At the **Bastion East Gate**, Berridin found guards operating a checkpoint. He disguised himself as an ordinary human, reasoning that a human would attract less scrutiny, and used his familiar to assist.

Exhaustion reduced the quality of the disguise far below his standards, but it was magnificently plain (**Deception 26 – 8 = 18**). He passed through and reached the bank with only hours remaining.

The first teller confirmed that the bank was preparing to repossess Lord Whitaker’s Estate. Berridin wanted to repay the loan but lacked proof that he represented the estate. The teller required the family sigil and warned that the bank would close in two hours.

Berridin contacted Witty through the sending stone. Witty did not know about a sigil, though he did have his family ring.

Rather than wait, Berridin returned inside and approached a different teller. He still lacked proof, but desperation slowly transformed a discussion of “what could be done” into an unmistakable bribe.

The teller asked for fifty gold. Berridin pleaded until he reduced the price to forty-nine.

Berridin emptied the bag of holding onto the counting tray. He paid **1,000 gold** to settle the loan in full and another **49 gold** as the teller’s “administrative fee.”

The teller counted everything, confirmed that the debt was settled, and presented a receipt.

**Berridin:** "And what is your name again?"

**Teller:** "Cornelius."

Berridin signed the receipt as **John Smith**.

Cornelius did not care.

## 9) The Little Hill Beside the Road

Far behind Berridin, the carriage continued toward Bastion. Witty, Ellara, and Nyx eventually noticed a majestic steed grazing beside the road—and a large, familiar shape asleep nearby (**Witty Perception 9; Ellara Perception 15; Nyx Perception 12**).

**Ellara:** "Is that... is that Master Ysidor?"

Nyx walked over and woke him.

**Ysidor:** "Uh, Din? Did you come back?"

**Nyx:** "No, it’s us. What happened? Where’s Din?"

**Ysidor:** "Din left. I fell off the horse, and he went on."

Berridin had already contacted Witty to confirm that the loan was handled. Rather than go to the Whitman residence, however, he had used a key previously lifted from Nyx and let himself into Nyx’s townhouse without permission.

Nyx contacted him from the road.

**Nyx:** "Din, are those my curtains I’m hearing? We’re here with Ysidor, and he’s asleep by the road. Care to explain?"

Berridin said that Ysidor “didn’t make it,” asked the others to say he was proud, and promised to have a hot meal waiting when Ysidor reached “our home.” He then asked where Nyx kept his food.

Nyx released the world’s deepest sigh.

**Nyx:** "Are you telling me that you had a sending stone and you didn’t think to call?"

Berridin blamed the exhaustion and complained that Nyx’s curtains did not properly block the daylight. Ysidor, still drifting in and out of sleep, admitted that he had hit his head when he fell but thought he was fine.

**Berridin:** "Let’s not make a mountain out of a Little Hill right now."

Nyx directed Berridin to a hidden food stash and ordered him to remain out of sight. Berridin owed Ysidor, and Nyx did not want anyone seeing him enter or leave the townhouse.

**Nyx:** "Lock the front door. I swear to god, if you leave it open, I will kill you."

Berridin apologized—and then mentioned that some things had apparently been broken before he arrived. He had tried unsuccessfully to put them back together.

Nyx added one final boundary: Berridin was not to touch his absinthe unless he had a death wish.

The carriage party recovered Ysidor and continued to Witty’s house. With the Whitman residence safe from repossession, Nyx instructed Berridin to leave the townhouse and join them.
