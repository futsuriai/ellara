# Session 22 — *An Enchanted Evening*

**Locales:** Whitman Estate (Bastion City) · Duskbreaker Lighthouse (front desk) · Eulogian Cloister (Proctor Eduard's office) · Bastion City streets & market

**Time:** Evening → midnight → following morning → afternoon & evening

---

## 1) Plotting the Pitch — The Whitman Estate

Back in Bastion City, Ellara returned to her cloister dorm to weigh Proctor Eduard's writ of passage for the train to Eulogian headquarters in **Pharus**, the imperial capital in Ordonne. Meanwhile, the rest of the party regrouped at **Whitaker "Witty" Whitman VI's** house to figure out their next move—specifically, how to get Witty into **Michelle Fabergé's** vacant position at the **Duskbreaker Lighthouse**.

Witty's parents had left a note saying they were currently in the capital. Berridin asked whether they needed more money or should rob Witty's parents, to which Witty replied he could always ask them—or earn his own money by landing the job.

**Witty:** "We're all in agreement me getting this job is a foregone conclusion. I'm a shoo-in."

There was no public job posting for Michelle's old role. **Donnathan Reeves**, Director of Public Relations for the Duskbreaker Lighthouse—whose office sat right beside Michelle's—was a Viscount, a known lover of the Grand Duke, and an old friend of Witty's older brother. Before moving forward, Nyx considered whether Michelle's death was common knowledge so Witty wouldn't look suspicious pursuing her spot (**History 13**): they had been gone over a month and her body had been found. While it wouldn't have been announced in the streets, there would have been an obituary in the papers and talk in upper circles, so it was no secret. She was survived by her cat, siblings, and a few nieces and nephews.

Berridin, convinced Donnathan was "bad and all aboard with the Lighthouse," proposed disguising the rest of the party as rival applicants who would bomb their interviews so badly that Witty won by default. Witty doubted Berridin would pass as a believable candidate, and Nyx shot the idea down outright—there was no open posting to apply to, too many points of failure, and a sudden flood of applicants would look suspicious.

**Witty:** "I present myself as a useful idiot that Donnathan feels like he can control and have his person in the position. I'd be a pretty good pawn."

**Berridin:** "So you acknowledge your place in this, Witty."

**Ysidor:** "Now we're talking."

Since Donnathan already knew Witty—and Witty genuinely wanted to be a scientist—they scrapped the fake-applicant scheme and decided to simply get Witty and Donnathan in the same room.

---

## 2) The Mystery of the Mail & A Late-Night Delivery

Getting a letter to Donnathan proved unexpectedly complicated.

**Witty:** "I can have my secretary write a letter to his secretary inviting me to lunch."

**Berridin:** "I know you're talking about me. I barely understand what secretary means, and I don't know how to send a letter."

Neither did Witty. Nyx only used his own network of couriers and dead drops, and promptly dismissed Witty's suggestion of a pigeon—a hawk would eat it. The group racked their brains over how ordinary citizens sent mail:
* **Witty (Intelligence 10)** vaguely remembered Jarvis handling it, never being gone long, and possibly mentioning a mailman.
* **Ysidor (Intelligence 7)** had no idea.
* **Nyx (Intelligence 15)** pieced it together: Bastion's postal system worked much like dead drops. In the western side of the city, nobles' addresses were known, so you simply wrote their name and title and left the letter in the household mailbox for the mailman to collect and deliver (with richer houses having gates and guards). On the east side, you needed a description of where the recipient lived, and letters went to public drop boxes unless you paid extra for direct handoff.

Frustrated, Nyx told Witty he would handle delivering the letter *just this once* and to write it on good paper. Witty sat at his desk with a purple peacock-feather quill and purple wax seal to compose an ornate invitation. Nyx stepped outside and placed it in the little brass mailbox by Witty's door—leaving Berridin amazed that houses came with mailboxes.

Because it was already evening, no mailman would collect it until morning. Ysidor stepped outside to check the box and returned visibly disappointed.

**Ysidor:** "The letter's still there. It didn't magically go anywhere."

**Nyx:** "Adorable. The mailman has to pick it up tomorrow morning when they drop off mail."

**Berridin:** "That's what those guys walking around the city with those bags do. That's amazing."

Witty and Berridin thought waiting was too passive, and Nyx agreed to drop it off at the Lighthouse himself tonight to save time and stage-set Witty's approach. Raiding Witty's closet with Berridin's help, Nyx assembled a standard courier uniform (**Deception with advantage: 5, then 22**), aided by the gathering dark.

Crossing the light-crystal-lit bridge late at night, Nyx noted the heightened security since their earlier incident: large modern warforged now stood stationed outside alongside regular guards. Inside, a single bored receptionist sat at the front desk.

**Receptionist:** "Good evening, sir. Suppose you have some mail for us? Awfully late, guess it's someone rather important."

**Nyx:** "Oh yes, tell me about it. Can't wait to get home tonight, been one hell of a day."

**Receptionist:** "Oh, those lords. Up all hours, they don't really care."

**Nyx:** "No, they don't." *(hands over the letter)* "They paid handsomely for this."

**Receptionist:** "Oh, for Director Reeves. Unsurprising. I'll make sure it's on his desk in the morning when he arrives. Your client will hear soon—he's fairly quick to respond, though he's a busy man."

Nyx walked out with a casual hurry as the receptionist set the letter aside for morning delivery.

---

## 3) Short Notice, Rotten Food, and Honest Apologies

Nyx returned to the Whitman estate around 9 or 10 p.m. and reported the letter delivered. When he finally asked what Witty had actually written, Witty dropped a bombshell: he had invited Donnathan to dinner **tomorrow night**, promising an upscale menu including foie gras and assuming Nyx—whom he'd heard was a good chef—would pull it together.

**Nyx:** "You didn't give him any time, you didn't give us any time. What if he reads it tomorrow afternoon or night and we miss our shot?"

**Witty:** "I gave us an entire day. That's more time than I usually give my servants to prepare for a meal."

**Nyx:** "Donnathan Reeves is not your servant."

**Witty:** "I heard you're a good chef, Nyx."

**Nyx:** "I'm not a miracle worker."

While they argued over the menu, **Ellara** arrived in person, still mulling over Proctor Eduard's offer and the train writ.

**Ellara:** "What's happening here?"

**Berridin:** "We're going to throw a very decadent feast for this Lighthouse Nazi guy, and we're going to get Witty installed in Michelle Fabergé's former position at the Lighthouse."

**Nyx:** "So we can find out more about the process of turning Shadowed into these stones, these energy sources."

Berridin pitched an unethical menu of rich-people delicacies—foie gras, sea turtle soup, shark fin soup, balut, and veal—while Nyx insisted that if he was cooking, he would decide the menu. Checking Witty's kitchen after a month of vacancy, Nyx found staples decently stocked, no icebox magic, and every fresh ingredient either gone or rotten. Cursing over the spoiled food, he turned to Ellara to ask if she was okay; she answered that it had been a challenging night, but she felt she had gained some clarity.

Ellara then asked **Ysidor** for a private moment. Nyx busied himself to give them space.

**Ellara:** "I feel like I owe you an apology. During the ritual, I called Meri's words hearsay and that was unfair of me. She raised you and protected you—she is to you like Proctor Eduard is to me. You all have been speaking of him the same way I spoke of her, and it hurts."

She told Ysidor she had gone to speak with Proctor Eduard the day before—without giving any of their names or exact details—and that he had reacted with horror, kindness, and support, explaining that Reeves had been shutting him out and offering to send her to Pharus out of concern. She had come back to tell everyone herself so they wouldn't hear it from anyone else. Then she offered a second confession: during the ritual, after Ysidor asked her not to finish her blessing, she had finished it in secret, afraid that Hýr's light and blessing wouldn't be enough.

Ysidor scooped Ellara up in a massive hug, lifting her clean off the ground and swinging her.

**Ysidor:** "It's okay, I accept your apology and I'm sorry if I acted wrong. If you trust Proctor Eduard like I trust Meri, I trust him too, because you're my friend and I trust you."

Ellara teared up and hugged him back as best she could, marveling at how long it had taken her to see the mirror between Meri and Eduard. Ysidor admitted he was nervous about cooking for a stranger and suggested inviting Proctor Eduard to the supper—adding helpfully, *"You can always put paper in the metal box outside and it magically gets to the person."* (Across the house, Nyx sneezed.) Ysidor finally realized Ellara's feet were still dangling in the air and set her gently back down.

---

## 4) Inviting the Proctor & The "Enchanted Forest" Pivot

Ellara pitched Ysidor's idea to the group: inviting Proctor Eduard might help draw more insight out of Reeves and let the others meet him firsthand. To her slight surprise, both Witty and Nyx agreed—Nyx only asked that Ellara brief Eduard beforehand so it wasn't out of the blue.

They settled on a tight two-on-two guest list: **Witty, Ellara, Proctor Eduard, and Donnathan Reeves** at the table, with Berridin and Ysidor (too notorious to be seen as themselves) disguised as the help and Nyx working as the chef. Before leaving, Ellara asked Nyx to lay out the plan clearly so she could introduce it to Eduard. Nyx explained the goal of getting Witty into Michelle's spot—and asked Witty if there was anything else he'd put in the letter.

**Witty:** "I told him my brother was in town, his old friend, and that he was going to be here to see him, and we'd have a wonderful dinner of delicacies including his favorites from his childhood region."

**Nyx:** "Just to be clear, that's news—you didn't say that the first time. So the brother has to be here too."

**Witty:** "Not necessarily. He just thinks he's going to be here."

Nyx retreated to the kitchen to inventory ingredients and plan the menu during his trance. Around midnight, Ellara returned to the cloister, spotted flickering candlelight outside Proctor Eduard's office, and knocked lightly.

She told Eduard she had learned Donnathan Reeves would be dining at the Whitman estate tomorrow and offered to secure him an invitation so Reeves couldn't keep eluding him. Eduard agreed it wouldn't hurt to be in the same room to arrange a proper talk, noting he remembered some of the Whitman siblings from the ball—*"quite a large family, very unique in their colorings."* He offered to prepare a carriage for the evening, then warmly teased her.

**Eduard:** "I hope the young Whitman is very kind to you, Ellara. No need, I know sometimes your first crush gets you a little embarrassed… you can't control what the heart wants."

**Ellara:** *(grabbing her temple)* "There are so many things wrong with what you just said, but perhaps that is a discussion for not this late an hour."

---

## 5) Market Runs, Leaf Costumes, and Arrival of the Guests

The next morning, Nyx recruited Ysidor to haul groceries from the market (*"You are large and strong, can you help me?"* — Ysidor perked up immediately: *"Yes, I am."*). Back at the estate, Witty did morning yoga while Berridin took charge of prepping the house as maître d'. Over the sending stone, Berridin assured Nyx everything was under control—right before Witty shouted from the next room:

**Witty:** "Oh, by the way, it's enchanted forest themed."

Nyx called right back to demand an explanation. Witty insisted he had told Donnathan to dress for the occasion and expected "enchanted forest decorum"—whimsical fairies, gnomes, and matching food, decorations, and service to uphold House Whitaker's renowned themed parties. Resigned, Berridin told Nyx he would handle the house.

Ellara finished her morning prayers (having left her sending stone in her room and missed the entire panic) and arrived to help. The party divided the work:
* **Decorating (Berridin with advantage, 2d100: 48):** With Ysidor reaching the high spots, they dressed the large mansion with tree branches and dark-forest whimsy—clearly decorations, but on-theme.
* **Cooking (Nyx with advantage from Ellara, 2d100: 70):** Nyx stuck to reliable, upscale dishes he knew well, filling the kitchen with an excellent aroma.
* **Witty** changed into an immaculate tailored purple suede suit with a cane and plumed hat, spent 45 minutes micro-adjusting in the mirror, and then came downstairs to slightly rearrange Berridin and Ysidor's decorations.

When the first knock sounded that evening, Berridin vetoed Witty's suggestion of garden-gnome outfits—pointing out that gnomes were real and Bastion's elite were deeply prejudiced toward Shattered people. Instead, Berridin disguised himself and Ysidor as scantily clad human "tree nymphs" with fake pointy ears and strategically placed leaves, Adam-and-Eve style: Berridin as a beautiful human woman, and Ysidor as a 6'5" big human ("Bear," since "Moose" was compromised) wearing a very small leaf and completely unbothered. Nyx disguised himself as a nondescript human chef in a white coat and tall hat.

**Proctor Eduard** stood at the double doors in his Eulogian priest robes. Berridin greeted him and offered to take off his ceremonial robes (*"Pardon me, that's rather forward. No, we'll keep this on."*). Eduard remarked that it had been a long time since he'd seen an enchanted forest theme, and mentioned having met Witty's father in passing and believing one of the Whitman children belonged to the Eulogia. Watching his reaction, Berridin gauged that Eduard wasn't hiding anything—he simply found the theme quaint (**Insight**).

In the dining room, Witty turned dramatically from a tall window and launched into full schmooze mode, presenting himself as a burgeoning scientist making a name for himself far from home. Eduard remarked that Ellara was like a daughter to him and seemed taken with Witty; Witty awkwardly replied he was trying to "give her companionship" and help her break out of her shell, then leaned back and yelled *"Ellara!"*

Ellara emerged from the kitchen, saw Berridin standing to the side in leaves—one of which casually dropped to the floor right after he winked at her—and froze mid-sentence. Eduard gently waved it off, noting it wasn't the first themed noble party he had attended.

---

## 6) Dinner with the Viscount — Offers, Power Plays, and Warnings

A second knock brought **Donnathan Reeves**, impeccably dressed in an ornate art nouveau formal jacket with hints of purple. Alone at the door, Ysidor gave an awkward bow copied from Ellara, took his overcoat, and simply pointed toward the dining hall. Donnathan flirted lightly (*"You sure are handsome"*), concluded he was new and *"hired for certain talents, not others,"* and showed himself in.

Entering the dining room, Donnathan greeted Eduard with mild surprise, complimented Witty's "aesthetic" taste in servants—teasing about "Jarvis 2.0" and pointing out Berridin's slipped leaf so Berridin had to pull it back up—and told Eduard the Grand Duke had been demanding, promising to make time later this week *"given recent incidences."* When Witty slipped and called Berridin "Din," Ellara smoothly covered with *"Thank you, Eve."* Berridin collected Ysidor from the front door, and the two began serving Nyx's multi-course dinner (preceded by Witty's confident promise of *"oars devours"*).

Seated at the table, Donnathan asked why Witty had actually invited him. Witty came straight to the point: he wanted to replace Michelle Fabergé despite his lack of qualifications.

**Donnathan:** "Right to the point—not like your brother."

Turning to Eduard, Donnathan confirmed they no longer had a Director of Research, hadn't been actively looking yet, and knew the Eulogians had opinions.

**Eduard:** "We want to ensure you have a proper Eulogian. While Director Fabergé was proper, there was whispering of not so proper, so Eulogia is concerned about the next one."

Donnathan agreed and ruled Witty out for the director role. Witty abruptly pivoted to suggest Ellara as a Eulogian representative, catching her completely off guard until Eduard stepped in and Ellara nervously clarified she had never expressed interest in the role.

Donnathan, however, had a counteroffer. Because the Director of Research role was under scrutiny between the Grand Duke and the Eulogians, the Lighthouse wanted to send **an envoy to the capital (Pharus)** to represent Lighthouse interests and scout **Torchlight College** nearby for candidates. It came with a handsome stipend, first-class train passage, housing, a spending allowance, and an event budget with no crude reimbursement paperwork.

Witty immediately asked whether Ellara could accompany him. Eduard chimed in that he had already suggested Ellara go to Pharus for her Eulogian education—the political seat of the church being in Pharus, even if mythos held it was born in Bastion—while gently assuring Ellara she shouldn't let Witty pressure her.

Impressed by the food (even if the wine wasn't up to his family's vintage), Donnathan brushed past Witty's attempt to take credit and waved Ysidor to fetch the chef. Nyx emerged in disguise and bowed (**Deception 21**).

**Donnathan:** "Thought you looked familiar—guess I'm wrong. Witty, you attract handsome employees, quite to my taste. Excellent meal, chef. If you ever need a job, hit me up."

**Nyx:** "You are far too kind, thank you."

**Donnathan:** "I could be kinder."

Donnathan waved Nyx away at the exact same time as Witty, not even glancing at the host. Over the course of the evening, the party tried to read the Viscount:
* **Witty** believed Donnathan was simply acting like a caring older brother.
* **Ysidor** and **Berridin (Insight 13)** picked up nothing unusual.
* **Ellara (Insight Dirty 20)** saw right through him: Donnathan treated Witty's house as if it were his own, asserted superior rank by dismissing Nyx without checking with Witty, was cocky and controlling, and thought very little of Witty while barely bothering to hide it.

Donnathan told Witty to come by the Lighthouse tomorrow to sign the paperwork and contract for the temporary position. Eduard whispered to Ellara that he had already arranged to speak with Donnathan later this week, away from prying ears, and offered her a ride back in his carriage—or to stay the night at her "companion's." Ellara immediately chose the carriage.

After Eduard and Ellara left, Witty insisted Ellara would warm up to him. Donnathan remarked that *"not everyone likes to play with fire like me"* and mentioned he was heading to see the Grand Duke. When Witty offered to send one of his servants (*"anyone but the cook"*) to escort him, Donnathan's tone turned sharp.

**Donnathan:** "Be very careful what you're offering. Having someone's servant told to do that isn't a pleasure I seek—I'd rather them be of free will. I hope you don't take advantage of your servants. I'd advise your family if you did."

Retrieving his coat from Ysidor ("tall one"), Donnathan thanked Witty for the evening, said he'd see him tomorrow, and departed in his carriage.

Meanwhile, in Eduard's carriage, the Proctor dropped his teasing and gently advised Ellara to be careful around young Lord Whitman. Ellara placed a hand on his shoulder and told him earnestly that for *"many, many reasons,"* Witty was not someone she would ever be romantically involved with—though she believed he was a genuine friend in his own way. Eduard accepted her discernment.

---

## 7) Aftermath — Road Trip to the Capital

Once both guests were gone, Nyx peeked out from the spotless kitchen, confirmed the coast was clear, and shed his chef disguise.

**Nyx:** "How'd that go?"

**Witty:** "Not bad. Have you ever been to the capital?"

**Nyx:** "Yes, I have."

**Witty:** "Perfect. We're going on a road trip."

Witty relayed the outcome: he wasn't qualified to replace Michelle Fabergé, but he had landed a Lighthouse envoy role in Pharus—meaning the rest of the party would be relocating as his retinue once they saw the contract details tomorrow.

**Berridin:** "We'll get the details. There's no way Witty could screw this up now, right?"

**Witty:** "No, never."

Turning around, Nyx realized Ellara was already gone.

**Nyx:** "Where's Ellara?"

**Berridin:** "We let her go with the crazy priest, man."

**Nyx:** "When you put it that way it sounds terrible, but okay."

Nyx decided to reach out to Ellara later, once she was no longer with Proctor Eduard.
